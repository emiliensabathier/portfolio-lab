Risk contribution

Asset class attribution

Where the return came from, and where the risk sits. The two columns are computed on different bases and are not a like-for-like decomposition: return contribution is summed over the whole backtest, while share of risk is a snapshot of the final target allocation only. A bloc can therefore show a large return contribution and zero risk share simply because it was held for years and then dropped from the current weights. Unlike every other figure in this report, the return contributions here are computed from raw asset returns and are not net of transaction costs.
